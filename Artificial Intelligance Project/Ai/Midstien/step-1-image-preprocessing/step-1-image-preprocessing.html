2. Simulated preprocessing pipeline

Before OCR, real systems clean the image to make characters easier to recognise. Here we simulate three common steps and explain why each one is important.

Conversion to grayscale

Waiting

The colour image is converted into shades of gray. This removes colour information that is not relevant for recognising text, reduces data size, and simplifies later processing.

Noise reduction

Waiting

Small random dots or scan artefacts are reduced. This prevents the OCR engine from confusing noise with punctuation or mathematical symbols.

Contrast enhancement

Waiting

The difference between the background and the text is increased so that numbers, operators, and symbols stand out more clearly for OCR.

Note: In this project, we only simulate these steps conceptually. A production system would operate on pixel values and produce a new, cleaned image.
