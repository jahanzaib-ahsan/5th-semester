1. Why user validation matters

Even advanced OCR and AI models can misread symbols, especially in mathematical notation. In safety-critical or academic systems, a user is often asked to confirm the recognised text before it is used.

  • Small OCR mistakes can completely change the meaning of an equation.
  • Users may want to add missing context, such as units or constraints.
  • Confirmation creates a clear responsibility boundary between machine and human.
